# Code of conduct

Cyron follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be respectful and patient. Assume good intent and explain, rather than dismiss.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, sexualized content, or publishing someone else's private information.
- Keep discussions on topic and help newcomers find their way.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space, and when you represent the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainer, [@Ry0x7](https://github.com/Ry0x7), privately. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, lock threads, or block people who break this code, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
